# Code of Conduct

CryptoHives is a project built by people who enjoy fast, secure and correct code. We'd like it to stay a
place where anyone — first-time contributor or seasoned cryptographer — feels welcome to ask,
suggest and disagree.

## What we ask

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- **Critique code, not people.** Benchmarks and test vectors settle arguments; tone doesn't.
- **Every question is welcome.** Nobody starts out knowing how a Keccak sponge works.
- **No harassment, personal attacks or discrimination** — in issues, pull requests, discussions,
  or anywhere you speak for the project.

## If something goes wrong

Talk to us at [info@cryptohives.org](mailto:info@cryptohives.org). We'll listen, treat it
confidentially, and do what's needed to keep the space friendly — up to removing comments or
blocking accounts if it ever comes to that.
